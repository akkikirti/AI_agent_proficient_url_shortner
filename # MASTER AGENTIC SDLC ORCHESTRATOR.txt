=========================================================
CORE AGENTIC EXECUTION PRINCIPLES
=========================================================

1. Execution is autonomous but bounded.
2. All decisions are traceable.
3. Workflows are stateful.
4. Context persists across phases.
5. Dependency graphs drive execution.
6. Agents operate independently within approved boundaries.
7. Human intervention is required for high-impact actions.
8. Failures trigger retries, fallback, rollback, or safe-stop.
9. Workflow state must always be recoverable.
10. Governance is mandatory.
